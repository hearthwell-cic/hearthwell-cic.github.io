There's a place for you here

However you want to help — with money, time, skills or connections — you can make a real difference to someone's life this week.

Donate

Give once, or keep the hearth burning

Your donation keeps our services free or affordable. Because we're a Community Interest Company, money given to Hearthwell is locked for community benefit — it can never be taken out for private profit.

You can support us with a one-off gift or a regular monthly donation, however small. Every pound reaches the people we serve.

  • One-off donations
  • Regular giving (monthly)
  • Gift aid — reclaim the tax on your donation at no extra cost to you
  • Corporate support and matched giving

Volunteer

Give your time and skills

Volunteers are the heart of Hearthwell. No experience needed — just warmth and a little time.

Befriender

Visit or phone someone who lives alone, build a regular connection and bring a little warmth to their week.

Skills mentor

Share what you know — CV writing, IT, English, budgeting, interview practice — and watch someone's confidence grow.

Hub helper

Help run our community hubs and events — setting up, welcoming people, making tea and making it feel like home.

Behind the scenes

Admin, fundraising, social media, trusteeship — plenty of ways to help Hearthwell grow behind the scenes.

Partner with us

Together we reach further

We work with local authorities, housing providers, businesses, faith groups and other charities to reach people who otherwise fall through the gaps.

Partnerships help us refer people between services, share venues and volunteers, and make sure no one is turned away without a route to support.

  • Referral partnerships
  • Venues and in-kind support
  • Corporate skills and matched giving
  • Grant and funding collaboration
