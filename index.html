Our mission

Belonging, skills and support for people who have been left out

Across the UK, millions of people cannot afford the support they need — befriending services, training courses, family help. They fall through the gaps between public services, charities and paid providers.

Hearthwell fills those gaps. We deliver practical, affordable and human-centred support, so that a person's circumstances never decide whether they get the help they need.

What we do

Support that meets people where they are

Four strands of work, one goal: helping people feel connected, capable and supported.

Community connection

Befriending, community hubs and social groups that end loneliness and help people belong.

Skills & employment

Practical training and coaching that help unemployed adults rebuild confidence and find work.

Family support

Affordable help for low-income families and single parents — from food support to budgeting and advocacy.

Support for older people

Home visits, befriending and practical help for older people living alone and feeling forgotten.

Who we help

We prioritise people who have the fewest options

Anyone is welcome to our services. In practice, we focus our support on those who cannot afford paid alternatives.

  • Low-income families
  • Unemployed adults
  • Older people living alone
  • Single parents
  • Vulnerable communities
